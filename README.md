# 客户生日簿 · Customer Birthday Book

A phone app for remembering customers' birthdays and sending wishes on WhatsApp.

- **Today's reminder**: how many birthdays are today, in the next 1–3 days, and missed in the last 7 days.
- **Birthday cards** in 4 designs, drawn on the phone, with English, Malay or bilingual wishes.
- **Send one by one**: share the card and the wish to WhatsApp in one tap (on phone browsers that support sharing), then move to the next customer.

## Privacy

This site contains **no customer data**. When you first open it, you choose your own Excel file
(columns `Customer Name` and `Birthday`, optional `Phone`). The list, the "sent" marks and the phone numbers
are kept **only in that phone's browser**. Nothing is uploaded.

## Use it

Open the site on your phone, then add it to the home screen:
- iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen
- Android (Chrome): ⋮ → Add to Home screen
